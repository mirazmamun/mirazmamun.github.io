Counting 4 since my dad died

Time puts a bandaid on the wounds. I recall the first actute agony I felt when I lost my dad. The news seemed to be
crushing in, engulfing me in a wave of grief. The feeling that I won’t be able hear him talk, see him in front, feel his presence
in the room. It was a feeling of emptiness, helpless yearning to hold him back from the journey of un-returning.

Over the years, daily grinds has taken over. Those pains were alleviated by the daily routine of work, family, and friends.
But at moments, it came with sharp awakened pain, overwhelming me. Those unsurmountable momments would sway the way had been seeing life.

This day, I am fonding the memories of him again. They day we lost him. As time passes, I fear that I the whisper of his voice
is getting fainter. I hope that this writing would be a reminder to me, to keep his memory alive.

My day was nothing special to the society. Just a regular person. But to me, his simplicity, his way of living spartan life, the
insolvency of the family, the struggle but rewarding journey during our upbringing. I am incarnation of the hard and loving
lifestyle we cherished.
