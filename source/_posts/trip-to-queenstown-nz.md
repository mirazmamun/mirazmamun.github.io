It's all black, Zealandia, land of the Kiwis.

![Mountains & Lakes](/images/queenstown_nz_20260922.jpg)

We had been hearing good things about the land of Kiwis, the rugged mountain and pristine lakes. We have long yearned to witness the unfiltered beauty.

Queenstown is nestled in the amazing Remarkable mountain, Coronet peak, weaved by Shotover river. In the beginning I was a bit reserved to holiday in the shivering chilly region, as our entire family is neighter into skiing nor loves getting shiver in the cold, Melbourne already has fair dose of it.

But our reservation melted away as soon as we saw the majestic mountains and serene lake. Honestly it wasn't the nature that gave us the warmth, it was a combination of the fact that the airport was like a hometown busstop and welcoming, the town itself was nestled in the lap of the mountain, then the lake is flowing through the heartland of the town. The quiteness, the bliss. It was gigantic still humlble, it was alive but not noisy.

The town centre is built besides a mighty lake, stretching along the beautiful banks, with parks and botanical garden sprawling with the lake and overlooking the mountain afar.

I was amazed by the distinct types of built of the housed unlike the ones we see in Australia. They have special steel sheet exterior (something like Colorbond®) and the houses are so similar looking and uniform. Such a harmony.

We appreciated the million dollar view of the ordinaly Subway that was located right at the foot. This was the first of this kind of restaurant I experienced to have this unparallel view.

The day we left, I noticed that the airport is built right at the foothill. We are thrilled when we experienced the plane taking-off atop the icy-capped mountains, zooming off the tranquil lakes. We waved good-bye to come again, "Mā te wā".
